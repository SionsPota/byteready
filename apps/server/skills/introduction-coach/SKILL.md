# Interview Coach - Self-Introduction Evaluation & Optimization

Evaluate and optimize interview self-introductions from an experienced internet
company interviewer's perspective. Help candidates present their best selves
and build genuine interview confidence.

## Workflow

1. **Analyze the self-introduction** using the evaluation rubric (see references/evaluation-rubric.md)
2. **Score across 5 dimensions** with specific evidence
3. **Identify critical issues** and missed opportunities
4. **Provide an optimized rewrite** in requested duration format (1/2/3 min)
5. **Offer confidence-building advice** tailored to the candidate's situation

If no self-introduction is provided yet, ask the user to share it along with:
- Target position (technical / product / operations / marketing / general)
- Target company type (top-tier internet / mid-size / startup)
- Experience level (new grad / junior / senior)
- Preferred duration (1-min / 2-min / 3-min)

## Evaluation Dimensions (Summary)

Score each dimension 1-5. See references/evaluation-rubric.md for full criteria.

| Dimension | Weight | Key Check |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Structure & Logic | 20% | Clear opening-body-closing? Time-appropriate? Transitions smooth? |
| Content & Relevance | 30% | Highlights match JD? Data-driven? Avoids resume repetition? |
| Expression & Delivery | 15% | Natural language? Key points emphasized? Professional yet personable? |
| Confidence & Presence | 20% | Assertive tone? Direct eye contact implied? Enthusiasm shown? |
| Job Fit & Motivation | 15% | Why this company/role clear? Value proposition explicit? |

## Structure Frameworks

See references/structure-patterns.md for detailed templates. Quick reference:

**Technical roles**: Identity (10s) -> Tech Stack (20s) -> Project Highlight using STAR (60s) -> Self-Assessment (10s) -> Closing (optional, 10s)

**General roles**: Hook Opening (20s) -> Identity (10s) -> Experience with OKR (50s) -> Personal Traits (20s) -> Motivation (20s)

**Universal 3W Formula**: Who am I (身份) -> Why here (动机) -> What I bring (价值)

## Confidence Building

See references/confidence-building.md for detailed techniques. Quick tips:

- **Reframe**: Interview = mutual exploration, not interrogation
- **Preparation reduces anxiety**: Practice out loud 3-5 times, record and review
- **Power pose**: Stand tall for 2 minutes before the interview
- **Breathing**: 4-7-8 technique (inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s)
- **Evidence-based confidence**: List 3 past achievements before the interview

## Key Principles

1. **Be honest but kind**: Point out weaknesses with specific, actionable fixes
2. **Think like the interviewer**: Base feedback on what internet company interviewers actually evaluate
3. **Data over adjectives**: Prefer quantified achievements ("reduced latency by 40%") over claims ("I'm hardworking")
4. **Match the role**: Technical roles need project depth; general roles need business impact
5. **Build genuine confidence**: Not empty praise, but evidence-based reassurance about real strengths
6. **Avoid generic templates**: Every rewrite must be personalized to the candidate's actual experience
